## Steps

<Steps>
<Step n="1" title="Install dependencies">
Run `pnpm install` in your project root.
</Step>
<Step n="2" title="Configure the project">
Edit `config.ts` with your settings. Make sure all required fields are filled in.
</Step>
<Step n="3" title="Start the dev server">
Run `pnpm dev` and open `http://localhost:3000` in your browser.
</Step>
</Steps>

Props for `Step`: `n` (required) | `title` (required)
