## The Problem

Restaurants face a constant battle with traditional printed menus. Updating prices to reflect supply costs, adding seasonal dishes, or crossing off 86'd items mid-shift requires expensive reprints and ruins guest presentation. Furthermore, paper menus cannot answer guest dietary questions, suggest wine pairings, or translate unfamiliar culinary terms.

---

## The Solution

DineCard is an intelligent digital menu experience accessible in seconds by scanning a tabletop QR code. Built from the ground up for speed, visual clarity, and mobile responsiveness, it gives restaurateurs total control over their menus in real-time.

---

## Key Features

### 📱 App-Free QR Onboarding
Guests simply point their camera and browse immediately. No app downloads, accounts, or cumbersome registration barriers.

### ⚡ Real-Time Inventory & Pricing
Toggle items out-of-stock instantly from any smartphone. Update daily specials and prices with immediate reflection across all tables.

### 🤖 AI Pairing & Recommendations
Suggests complementary appetizers, beverage pairings, and house favorites based on guest preferences and dietary restrictions.

### 🌐 Dietary Filters & Accessibility
Instant filtering for vegan, gluten-free, halal, and allergen-sensitive diets, with multi-language translation.

---

## Results & Impact

Restaurants using DineCard have eliminated recurring printing costs, reduced menu maintenance to seconds, and improved table turn times by enabling guests to browse and decide on orders faster.
