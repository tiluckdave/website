Physical printed menus are frustratingly static. Whenever ingredient costs change or dishes run out mid-shift, restaurants either reprint stacks of paper or paste tape over prices. Most QR codes just link to a massive, unreadable 40MB PDF.

I built DineCard to make the dining menu a lightweight, real-time web application.

For diners, there are zero app downloads or accounts. Scanning the QR code loads a fast mobile page in under 400ms with clear dietary filters for vegan and gluten-free diets.

For the restaurant, staff can 86 (sold-out) any dish in two taps from their phone, and the update syncs immediately to all active tables.

The biggest challenge was handling flaky mobile connections in basement restaurants without dropping state, and keeping the staff dashboard dead simple so it can actually be used during busy peak hours.
