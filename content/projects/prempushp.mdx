## A Personal Project

Building something for your family hits in a way client work doesn't. Prempushp is my brother's food brand based in Akola, Maharashtra. Every time the site loads correctly or an order goes through, it feels personal.

Before this project, all customer inquiries, price negotiations, and product listings were handled manually over telephone calls and scattered WhatsApp chats. There was no single source of truth for inventory, pricing, or product specifications.

---

## What We Built

We developed a comprehensive two-tier architecture tailored to the exact workflow of a growing food enterprise:

### 1. Public Product Catalog (prempushp.in)
A clean, lightning-fast digital catalog optimized for mobile browsers. Displays detailed specifications, pack sizes, nutritional facts, and direct WhatsApp purchase integration.

### 2. Operations Dashboard (admin.prempushp.in)
A private administrative command center for the team to update product details, monitor inventory, manage inbound customer leads, and generate billing invoices without touching spreadsheets.

## Results & Impact

Prempushp transitioned from a purely local, word-of-mouth business into a digital-ready food brand. Customers across Maharashtra now share direct links to specific items, inquiries are processed faster, and manual catalog maintenance errors have been eliminated.
