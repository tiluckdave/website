Prempushp is my brother's food brand in Akola, Maharashtra. Building something for your family hits differently than client work.

As the business grew, all pricing, inventory, and orders were handled through endless phone calls, scattered WhatsApp voice notes, and messy spreadsheets. There was no single source of truth.

I built a full product suite for Prempushp: an ultra-fast mobile storefront (`prempushp.in`) for buyers and a private operations dashboard (`admin.prempushp.in`) for internal management.

Distributors browse the catalog on spotty mobile data and send structured orders straight to WhatsApp with one tap, matching how local commerce actually operates. The admin portal lets the team update pricing, track inventory, and generate GST invoices in seconds.

It eliminated the daily price confusion and gave the brand a clean, professional digital presence.
