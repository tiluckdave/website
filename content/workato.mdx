# Workato

<ByLine>_September 2024 - Present_</ByLine>

I joined Workato as an intern in the field connectivity team.

For my first year as an intern, I worked on building connectors using Workato's own SDK. It was a small team of 7-8 engineers shipping fast. Every connector is just translating how someone built their system and how customers expect to talk to it.

I also learned a lot about the platform, iPaaS, orchestration, and Workato.

Workato entered AI with its orchestration foundation. Product was built fast, evaluated fast, corrected fast and AI at workato 9 months ago was completely different than what it is today. Connectors easily became the backbone of the context AI needs.

With my internship ending, I also transiiotned into the platform team, working on more complex engineering problems and caring more about the infra, which led me the opportunity to build MCP servers.

Today, I build connectors, MCP servers and mess with AI at Workato, and I plan to continue doing so.
